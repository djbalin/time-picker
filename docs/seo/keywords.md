# Keyword research

Research date: 2026-10-15. Method: seed phrases, then web searches to see which pages rank and which questions come up (titles, H1s, "people also ask"-style questions), then judge competition from who ranks.

**Limitations, read first**

- **No search volumes.** No tool with real volume data was available, so every volume below is `unknown`. Rankings are based on intent and on who ranks today. Get real numbers from Google Keyword Planner, and after a few weeks from the Search Console "Performance" report, then revisit this file.
- **US-biased results.** The search tool is US-only. Danish evidence is weaker: Danish queries mostly returned English pages or nothing relevant. That itself suggests little Danish competition, but treat it as a hypothesis.

## What ranks today (competition evidence)

| Query | Who ranks | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| doodle alternative / free doodle alternative | AlternativeTo, listicles (dupple, toolradar, ahaslides, surveysparrow), competitor pages (zcal, schedulingkit, Rallly blog) | Hard. Established listicles and funded competitors. Getting **listed** in these pages beats trying to outrank them. |
| doodle alternative free no ads | schedulingkit.com/free-alternative/doodle, AlternativeTo | Medium. Few dedicated pages; a focused page can compete over time. |
| how to find a date that works for everyone | howbout.app blog, labnol.org, wearespin.com, small tools | Medium-low. Blogs and small apps, no giants. Good long-tail target for the homepage. |
| free date poll for party / friends | whenavailable.com, Set The Date, KDemos, PollUnit, App Store | Medium-low. Small tools. Casual/social intent matches the product's tone. |
| doodle alternativ gratis / datoafstemning | English listicles, AlternativeTo, one Dutch page | Low (US index). No Danish page targets it. |
| find en dato der passer alle | No relevant tool pages at all | Very low. Open opportunity. |
| planlæg møde med venner find dato | English pages only (When2meet, WhenAvailable) | Very low in Danish. |

Recurring themes in competitor pages and questions: "free", "no sign-up / no account", "no ads", "for friends / parties / trips", Doodle's free plan getting more limited (claimed by competitors; not verified against Doodle's own pricing page).

## Priorities

1. **Danish first.** Low competition means the Danish pages can rank within weeks of being indexed; the English head terms will take far longer.
2. **Long-tail English** ("find a date that works for everyone", "date poll for friends") over head terms.
3. **Backlinks and listings** matter more than on-page work for "doodle alternative": submit to AlternativeTo (https://alternativeto.net/software/doodle/), Product Hunt, and ask the listicle authors above for inclusion.
